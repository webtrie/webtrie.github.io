Privacy Policy

PDF Merger – Split & Convert  ·  Effective date: August 8, 2026  ·  Developed by Varyqon Studio

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the PDF Merger – Split & Convert app ("the App"), developed and maintained by Varyqon Studio, handles your information. By using the App you agree to the terms described below. The App is designed to process your files entirely on your device — nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

2. Information we collect

Personal data. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, or the contents of your documents.

Device information. Limited technical information — such as device type, operating system version, and anonymised crash logs — may be collected automatically to help us improve stability and performance.

File and photo access. The App requests permission to access files and photos on your device solely so you can select PDFs and images to merge, split, extract pages from, or convert. Selected files are processed locally and are never transmitted anywhere. You choose which files to share with the App each time; we do not scan or access your storage in the background.

Advertising data. The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect anonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID)) to serve relevant ads. This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Settings under Google > Ads.

3. How we use information

The limited information described above is used solely to improve app performance and reliability, and to display ads that keep the App free for users. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party beyond the ad partner described in Section 2.

4. Local processing — your files stay on your device

All PDF operations — merging, splitting (extracting pages), converting, and extracting — are performed entirely on your device. "Split" and "Extract" refer to the same feature: pulling selected pages out of a PDF. Your PDF files and images are never transmitted to any external server. Processed files are saved to your local device storage only, and only with your explicit action.

5. Third-party services

The App integrates with Google Mobile Services, including Google AdMob, which operates under Google's Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for the data practices of third-party services. We encourage you to review their policies directly.

6. Data security

Because all file processing is local and we do not store or transmit your documents, the risk of unauthorised access to your files through our service is minimal. We recommend keeping your device up to date with the latest security patches.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Effective date" at the top will be updated. Continued use of the App after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Varyqon Studio at: support@varyqon.com
